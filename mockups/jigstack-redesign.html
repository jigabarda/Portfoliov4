#process

How I work.

Four steps from the first call to a product in your users' hands.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through your goals, users, and budget. You get a written scope and a fixed quote.

    2–3 days

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, then polished screens in Figma that you can click through and comment on.

    About 1 week

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos on a live preview link, so you can watch the product take shape.

    2–6 weeks

  4. 04

    Launch

    Deployment, monitoring, and a handover with documentation. I stay available for fixes.

    Ongoing

Timelines are typical for a small to mid-sized project.
